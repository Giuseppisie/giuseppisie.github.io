About me

Artist turned tinkerer — I use visual storytelling to design better engineering.

My earliest workshop was a sketchbook: drawing faces, experimenting with texture and light, and telling stories with images. Art taught me to observe, simplify, and iterate — skills that translate directly to product design. I care about how things feel in someone’s hands and how a tiny detail can change an entire experience.

That creative curiosity nudged me toward mechanical engineering: I wanted to make things that both behave well and look like they belong in the world. I study mechanical engineering to learn how to design reliable systems and materials — and I minor in Visual Arts so my prototypes don't just function; they communicate.

I also minor in Computer Science — not because I'm chasing syntax, but because I want to speak the language of the people who build the software my hardware will live with. Knowing CS helps me collaborate with engineers and make designs that bridge hardware, firmware, and user experience.
