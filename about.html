About me

Why Mechanical Engineering

I love to build. I want to be the person who comes up with solutions — and just as importantly, understands why those solutions work. Engineering is a field where the learning never ends, and that constant discovery is what excites me most.

It amazes me how much science and design goes into something as ordinary as a laptop sitting on a desk, let alone the layers of systems required just to turn it on. I’m genuinely happy to be learning the “whys” of the world, and I want a career where I can contribute meaningfully by turning that understanding into real, usable products.

My biggest interest is product design, especially in smart technology. Modern tech fascinates me — the ability to combine advanced science, creativity, and usability into objects we carry in our pockets, wear on our wrists, or build into our homes. I’m drawn to product design because it lives at the intersection of building and listening: understanding a user’s needs, identifying a real problem, and then creating something thoughtful, functional, and genuinely exciting.

Why Visual Art

I’ve loved art from a young age, and the desire to create has always brought me immense joy. I see engineering as art projects — just with more numbers at the end of the day. Products don’t only need to function; great design requires an artistic eye to create something a user wants to use.

Visual art has trained me to think about form, texture, balance, and emotion. It’s taught me how to create things that look good, feel good, and work together holistically to solve a problem — sometimes one the user didn’t even realize they had.

Why Computer Science

I began taking computer science courses at Duke because computational systems are everywhere in modern engineering, and I want to understand them. I don’t necessarily want to be the person writing the Java program — but I do want to understand how code, computer architecture, and AI work so I can design products that integrate seamlessly with software.

I want to communicate thoughtfully and knowledgeably with software engineers, understand what’s possible, and design hardware that complements digital systems rather than fighting them.

I built this entire website using HTML and GitHub — technologies I had never worked with before. My curiosity and desire to understand how code works pushed me to learn enough to bring a design vision to life. That same curiosity is what drives me forward, and I’m excited to keep exploring the digital world alongside my engineering career.
