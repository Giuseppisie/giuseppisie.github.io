Nyakuza Foot Bridge — Eswatini, Africa

Project overview

Role: Quality Control Manager · Location: Eswatini · Year: 2024

I spent eight weeks in Eswatini working alongside a team of Duke classmates, Engineers In Action staff, local masons, and community members to design and construct the Nyakuza Suspended Footbridge—the longest pedestrian bridge in the country at 128 meters.

Prior to traveling in-country, I assisted with the bridge’s design during the academic year, participating in engineering review calls and collaborating on structural decisions. While on-site, I worked directly with community members to ensure construction quality and safety.

My role (brief)

As Quality Control Manager I oversaw inspection checkpoints during construction, verified concrete mix delivery and placement, monitored anchor and cable tensioning, and documented deviations from design. My partner and I coordinated with the Design and Construction Managers, along with the local masons to ensure alignment with the design drawings and maintained QC forms and concrete test records. We had to ensure that all community members were aware of the construction saftey criteria durring the build every step of the way.
