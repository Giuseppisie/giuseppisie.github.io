EGR 121 — Course summary

Course projects: introductory team-based prototyping, CAD, boards, and CNC

EGR 121 introduces first years to the process of team-based creative conceptualization, visualization, prototyping, and product realization. Students use computer-aided design tools to create custom circuit boards and CNC-machined components to produce prototype systems. Design concepts are introduced and supported through hands-on assignments and multi-phase design challenges.

Below are three assignments completed in the course (DC2–DC4). Click any project tile to open a focused view with the design rationale, implementation notes, and deliverables from your submitted memos.

Mousetrap Car — DC2
Mousetrap Car — DC2 (Quantum Sand Machine)
A timed design challenge to convert the potential energy in a single mousetrap into vehicle motion across a sandy test pit. Emphasis: maximize distance while respecting strict rules and a tight materials budget.
Happy Meal Toy — DC3
Happy Meal Toy — DC3 (Happy Zoo Train)
Team-designed, injection-moldable train toy with interchangeable cars and functional wheels. Focus on manufacturability, cost analysis, choking-hazard compliance, and sustainability choices for mass production.
SportBot — DC4 (Rube-Goldberg)
SportBot — DC4 (Rube-Goldberg Machine)
Four teams collaborated to build a single machine that incorporated electronic circuits. Your subteam contributed two themed mechanisms (Legally Blonde and Titanic), each with its own circuit and mechanical action.
