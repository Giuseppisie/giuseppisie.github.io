EGR 121 — Engineering Design & Communication

Course Overview

EGR 121 introduces first years to the process of team-based creative conceptualization, visualization, prototyping, and product realization. Students use computer-aided design tools to create custom circuit boards and CNC-machined components to produce prototype systems. Design concepts are introduced and supported through hands-on assignments emphasizing documentation, iterative testing, and teamwork.

DC2 — Mousetrap Car (Quantum Sand Machine)

A challenge to convert stored energy from a single mousetrap into motion across a sandy course. Our team iterated wheel geometry, lever length, and chassis shape (triangular chassis chosen) to maximize distance while keeping weight reasonable. We tested wheel materials and lever attachments to find the best tradeoffs for sand traction and torque transfer.

DC3 — Happy Meal Toy (Train)

Design for a mass-producible Happy Meal toy: a buildable train with interchangeable car bodies. Each team-member designed a unique plastic part; parts were engineered for injection molding, shelling to reduce material, and snap-fit / peg features for assembly and safe play.

DC4 — Rube Goldberg / SportBot (Group Module)

Collaborative Rube Goldberg machine connecting four subteams. Our subteam built two movie-themed modules (Legally Blonde and Titanic) that included custom circuits (555 timer motor control, sensor triggers) and mechanical housings that integrated into the larger machine.
