Personal Projects Abroad

Madrid, Spain · Fall 2026 · Independent Exploration

Studying abroad in Madrid has given me the opportunity to continue exploring engineering and design outside of the classroom. Throughout the semester, I am using my time abroad to investigate problems, experiment with ideas, and develop small independent projects.

This page serves as an evolving collection of those explorations. Some may be worked till completion, some may remain just an idea, yet my goal is to hold myself accountable to furthering my engineering thinking in daily life. Each project documents the problem, research, design process, iterations, and lessons learned as the work develops.

This page is a work in progress; feel free to dig around below!
