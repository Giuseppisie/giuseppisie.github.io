Personal Abroad

My Semester Abroad

Madrid, Spain · Fall 2026 · Personal Exploration

Studying abroad in Madrid has given me the opportunity to explore far beyond the classroom. Throughout the semester, I have been documenting the places I visit, the things I learn, and the experiences that shape my time in Spain and around Europe.

Rather than treating these experiences as a traditional travel diary, I am using this page as an evolving collection of small explorations. Each week highlights something different — from architecture and art to food, travel, culture, and the unexpected moments that have made living abroad feel different from simply visiting.

Click through the explorations below to see photos, sketches, observations, and reflections from my semester abroad.

Arrival in Madrid

This first exploration documents my arrival in Madrid and the process of beginning to build a life in a new city.

Week 02 — Madrid Architecture
Madrid architecture
Madrid Architecture

This exploration looks at the architecture I encountered while walking through Madrid and how the built environment changes from neighborhood to neighborhood.

I focused particularly on details that connect architecture to everyday use: facades, balconies, windows, plazas, materials, and the relationship between old and new buildings.
