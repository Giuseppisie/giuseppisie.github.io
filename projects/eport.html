About this ePortfolio

I learned HTML to build this site from scratch so I could make every design decision myself. I started in early December 2025 and have been iterating on it since — choosing colors, font sizes, and how each box is placed so the site shows my projects the way I want them shown.

The process included a lot of experimentation: lots of Google/YouTube tutorials, questions to ChatGPT, and hands-on trial-and-error. Building this site taught me a lot about layout, accessibility, and how a small design change can change the whole feel of a page.

I would love feedback. Please tell me what works well and what doesn’t so I can continue improving this site.
