Product Space @ Duke — Fellow

Program overview

Organization: Product Space · Chapter: Duke University · Role: Fellow

Product Space is a national student program that connects students interested in product management and product development with practitioners, career resources, and hands-on project experiences. Duke's chapter brings the national network to campus through weekly sessions, mentorship, and project-based learning for student fellows.

As a Product Space fellow I attend weekly meetings and fireside chats with experienced product managers and engineers from a broad range of companies. These conversations help me understand the breadth of product careers and how design, analytics, and engineering intersect in real product teams.

My involvement

Weekly fireside chats · Project-based product work (coming)

I attend weekly Product Space sessions at Duke which typically take the form of fireside chats and panels. Guests include product leaders and engineers from a wide array of companies and product areas — from digital workout equipment to ride-sharing, large platform companies, and language-learning apps. These sessions have featured representatives from companies such as:

  • Peloton / digital fitness hardware & software
  • Lyft — product & operations
  • Amazon — consumer product and platform teams
  • Meta — product strategy and growth
  • Duolingo — product for learning & engagement

Through these conversations I’ve been able to ask about problem discovery, roadmapping, cross-functional collaboration, and how product teams measure success. The talks are intentionally practical — speakers walk through real tradeoffs, prioritization, and how they validate product decisions with users and data.

In the second semester of the fellowship I will be working as a product manager on a student project with a small team. We'll apply customer discovery findings and early prototypes to define a minimum viable product and user tests. Tune back in for updates on the project progress and outcomes!
