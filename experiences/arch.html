Fencing Facility

This project explores how architecture can draw public attention to a sport that is inherently dynamic but often hidden from view. The design aims to lure the outside into fencing, making its movement and intensity visible rather than enclosed.

Within a constrained program, the project organizes circulation to separate public spectators and private athlete movement without relying on enclosed boundaries. Spectators are brought directly into elevated viewing areas, while more embedded pathways allow fencers to move discreetly from locker rooms to the competition floor.
